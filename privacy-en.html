At a glance

Your saved places are stored on your iPhone. When you enable Google landmark recognition, your selected photo is sent for analysis. Firebase helps secure requests and manage credits, and AdMob may show optional ads.

1. About ONDE

ONDE — Your saved places map is an iPhone app that helps you identify places in photos or screenshots and organize them on a personal map. Contact the app developer at mvtitton@gmail.com. This policy applies to the ONDE app and the services supporting it.

2. Information processed

  • Selected images, screenshots and links: ONDE accesses content you choose to import. Images and saved places may remain on your iPhone.
  • Place details: names, addresses, coordinates, notes and visited status that you save.
  • Photo location metadata: coordinates embedded in selected photos, when available, may help place searches. ONDE does not require continuous location tracking.
  • Anonymous technical identifiers: Firebase Authentication may generate an anonymous identifier to manage security, quotas and reward credits, without account registration.
  • Usage information: our backend may process analysis counters, reward transactions, timestamps and technical data for fraud prevention and operations.
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob may process device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics in line with user choices and its own policies.

3. Place recognition

When you enable and use Google Cloud Vision recognition, your selected image is securely sent to ONDE's Google Cloud/Firebase backend and forwarded to Vision. Results may include possible landmark names, confidence scores and coordinates. Identification can be incorrect and requires your confirmation. The backend is designed for immediate processing and does not deliberately save uploaded image copies in Firebase Storage or Firestore. Google documents that synchronous Vision image requests are processed in memory. Apple Vision and MapKit, and public place information when available, may also be used.

4. Firebase and rewarded ads

We use Firebase services, including anonymous Authentication, App Check, Cloud Functions and Firestore, to secure requests and manage quotas and credits. Google AdMob may offer optional rewarded ads, which you may choose to watch in exchange for extra analyses. Rewards are verified server-side. Advertising processing and personalization, where allowed, depend on applicable consent and privacy settings.

5. Purposes

We process information to identify requested locations, store saved discoveries on your device, organize places on maps, manage quotas and rewards, prevent abuse, maintain security and investigate technical failures. We do not sell your personal saved-place lists or photos to advertisers.

6. Third parties and international processing

Google and Apple may process information to provide the functions described here. Processing may occur outside Brazil under their terms and applicable requirements. Advertising technology partners may process data according to their own policies and your available privacy preferences.

7. Retention

Locally stored places and images remain on your device until deleted or the app is removed, subject to system backups. Technical logs, anonymous identifiers, usage counters and reward records kept through Firebase are retained as needed for operation, security, fraud prevention and legal obligations, then deleted or anonymized under operational retention practices. Providers may have their own log retention periods. Build artifact retention does not mean user images are retained for that period.

8. Your choices and rights

You may choose not to import images or watch rewarded ads, and may use manual place entry where available. Device permissions can be managed in iOS settings. When advertising consent is requested, you can make your choices through the provided form and revisit privacy options where required. For data access, correction or deletion requests within our control, contact mvtitton@gmail.com. Uninstalling the app may not immediately delete backend technical records or third-party records.

9. Security

We use security measures available through the relevant platforms, including protected connections and request integrity checks. No technical system is risk-free. Avoid submitting images containing unrelated sensitive or confidential information.

10. Children

ONDE is not specifically directed to children. Guardians should consider applicable age, advertising and privacy settings.

11. Updates

We may update this policy when the app, integrated services or legal requirements change. The current version and last updated date will be available on this page.

12. Contact and third-party policies

Contact: mvtitton@gmail.com.
